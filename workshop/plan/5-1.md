## 3. Unit 5's color code (defined here, kept for every Unit 5 deck, printed as the legend on the words slide)

Unit 5 counts, weighs and converts; its code colors the three quantities of the unit and keeps Unit 4's particle drawings:
- **Mass in grams**: `--warm` (amber). Scale readouts, gram chips, the "g" badge on a bag or a sample.
- **Amount in moles**: `--accent` (the site's teal-green). Mole chips, the "mol" badge, the left side of the conversion map.
- **Number of particles**: `--e` (violet). Particle counts, the "particles" badge, the right side of the map. (Unit 4 uses violet for electron dots; in Unit 5 an electron is drawn only on the per-mole slide, as a particle, so the two uses agree.)
- **A conversion factor** is a bridge drawn in `--muted`; the unit that cancels is struck through in `--bad`; the unit that remains is circled in the quantity's color.
- **Ions**: cation red (`--cat`) with "+", anion blue (`--an`) with "−", exactly as Unit 4. **Atoms**: CPK colors (shared part) with the symbol on every sphere; the caption "Atom colors show the element, not the charge" at 34 px or more, outside the canvas, wherever ions and atoms share a slide (slides 9, 18).
- **Coins**: nickel `--metal`; cent made before 1982 `--rope` (copper); cent made after 1982 a lighter mix of `--rope` and `--slide` with a gray core when cut. Each coin carries its year side ("before 1982", "after 1982") as a label, never only a color.
- Tokens from the shared head: `--warm`, `--accent`, `--e`, `--muted`, `--bad`, `--ok`, `--cat`, `--an`, `--metal`, `--rope`, `--card`, `--line`, `--ink`; both themes audited.
- Legend entries on slide 5: "grams (mass)", "moles (amount)", "particles (number)", "+ cation", "− anion", "atom (CPK color = element)".
