## 3. The unit's color code and the 4.5 additions

Unit 4 code (RULES 8, legend on the words slide, identical in every deck): cation red with "+", anion blue with "−", electron dots violet with "−", δ+ warm, δ− teal, metal gray, nonmetal green; CPK colors for atoms in ball-and-stick and space-filling drawings with the symbol on every sphere; intersecting translucent circles for a shared pair; "+" and "−" on every drawn ion and electron. Tokens: `--cat`, `--an`, `--e`, `--dplus`, `--dminus`, `--metal`, `--nonmetal`, `--ok`, `--bad`, `--warm`, `--accent` and the CPK table in `02b-shared.html`.

Additions for 4.5 (used identically on every slide and printed on the words slide legend):
- A bond within a molecule: a solid gray stick (ball-and-stick) or the 4.1 shared-pair lens (dot drawings).
- Every attraction between molecules is dashed. Dispersion: gray, thin, flickering on and off while the stage runs (steady under reduced motion). Dipole-dipole: dashed from the δ+ end (warm) to the δ− end (teal). Hydrogen bond: dashed, teal, heavier than the dispersion line, always drawn from an H to a lone pair.
- Lone pair: a translucent violet lobe (the reading draws it blue; the unit's decks keep violet for electrons).
- Delocalized electrons: violet dots with "−" (as 4.1's electron sea). Metal cations: gray with "+".
- Lit bulb: warm glow (`--warm`); dark bulb: `--muted`. Charged particles that can move get a short motion trail while the stage runs.
- The crack in the NaCl block and the "like charges side by side" pairs: `--bad`.
- Ions and atoms on one slide (slides 6, 15, 17, 18, 19): the caption "Atom colors show the element, not the charge" at 34 px or more.
