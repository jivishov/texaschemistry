## Findings
- **F1 (fidelity; for the next brief, 02a or 03).** The isotope picture on slide 7 draws the two copper isotopes in two sizes, as plan section 4 asked. Isotopes of one element have the same size; the picture plants the idea that the heavier one is bigger. The notes say the sizes stand for mass, which is a patch on a wrong picture. Decision: equal sizes, two shades, nothing else added. The plan's choice was the orchestrator's; the implementer flagged it correctly (open question 4). Reported to the teacher as a judgment call.
