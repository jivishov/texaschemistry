## Findings
- **F1 (fix, 01a). The measurement counts what the rule does not mean.** RULES 3 forbids visible cards, chips, badges, labels and text lines from intersecting, and canvas labels from cutting shapes or each other. Four classes of the 270 hits are artifacts of measuring with rectangles: round shapes as boxes (a label in the empty corner of a circle's box; 27 in 4.1, 10 in 4.2); inline sub- and superscript boxes reaching into the line above while the glyphs do not touch (3 in 4.1, 69 in 4.2) and inline-block formulas taller than their line (6 in 4.2); labels of far spheres in a `D.spheres` scene under nearer spheres, which the depth order draws correctly (4.1 s7, 4.2 s15); a label fully under an opaque shape drawn later, which is hidden and not a visible overlap (4.1 s16's small "Na"). Brief 01a refines the measurement so that what remains is what the rule means.
- **F2 (fix, 01a). Live stages are measured only at rest.** Plan section 10 item 1 also wants two moments with motion on; brief 01a adds that pass to the layout audit for frames whose visible animation reports a live stage.
