### B. The six animations (plan section 4; the plan's row is the spec, this list adds the measurable parts)
Every animation: `Deck.sim(name, factory)`; named stages; `set(stage, instant)` with tweens of 500 to 900 ms and an instant end state under reduced motion (`Deck.reduced()`); `get()`; `bounds()` the drawn rect; `units()` the sub-units listed; `resize`, `redraw`, `active`; `meta()`; both themes through the CSS tokens (`--cat`, `--an`, `--e`, `--dplus`, `--dminus`, `--metal`, `--nonmetal`, `--ok`, `--bad`, `--warm`, `--muted`, `--accent`), CPK from `D.chem.CPK` (add `Cl`, `F`, `Br`, `I`, `Si`, `Na`, `K`, `Ca`, `Cu` to a lesson-local table if the shared one lacks them; do not change `02b-shared.html` in this step; say in the report which symbols you needed); every label at least 36 px and every shape at least 60 px on the canvas; symbol on every sphere; text never cut at the host's edge (`outText` counter as the reference sims keep it); no raster images; no library.

1. **`brickBag`** (slide 2). Stages `models`, `shake`, `pour`, `words`. Three snap-together brick models (three or four bricks each, one color family per model, studs drawn) resting in a clear bag; a counter "Bricks snapped: N of N" at 36 px or more that never changes. `shake`: the bag rocks and the models tumble apart; no brick comes off. `pour`: the models slide onto a table and spread far apart, whole. `words`: label "a snap between bricks = a bond within" over one model and "models side by side = an attraction between" over a gap. Units: the bag (or the table in `pour`), each model, the counter, each label in `words`. `meta()`: `{models:3, bricks:N, snapped:N, stage}` with `snapped===bricks` at every stage.
2. **`meltScale`** (slide 3). Stages `three`, `melt`, `numbers`. A melting-point scale (−200 to 1800 °C, with a marked break below 0 if the layout needs it) with three solids as icons above and their particles below: snow (water molecules, CPK, in open rings), salt (Na⁺ red "+" 102 pm and Cl⁻ blue "−" 181 pm to scale), sand (Si and O network). `melt`: the snow's molecules leave their places and drift, each whole, while salt and sand stay; the snowman icon slumps. `numbers`: the label line "Melting point" and chips "snow 0.0 °C", "salt 801 °C", "sand near 1700 °C". Units: each solid's panel, the scale, the chip row. `meta()`: `{values:{snow:0.0, salt:801, sand:'near 1700'}, snowMoleculesIntact:true, saltMoved:false, sandMoved:false}` after `melt`.
3. **`threeSolids`** (slide 6). Stages `chlorine`, `within`, `between`, `salt`, `copper`. Three panels. Chlorine: Cl₂ molecules in ball-and-stick, CPK green, "Cl" on every sphere. `within`: the stick inside each molecule glows and the label "bond within" appears. `between`: dashed gray lines between neighboring molecules flicker (one still frame under reduced motion), label "attraction between (much weaker)". `salt`: a 3D NaCl block with `Deck.spheres` (Shannon radii, 282 pm spacing, red "+" and blue "−"), label "ionic bonds through the whole crystal, no molecules". `copper`: one layer of gray "+" cores in a sea of violet "−" dots (the 4.1 `electronSea` look), label "metallic bonds through the whole metal". Units: each panel, each label. `meta()`: `{molecules:n, sticksWithin:n, dashedBetween:m>0, naclRadii:[102,181], naclSpacing:282, copper:'delocalized electrons'}`.
4. **`attractions`** (slides 7, 8, 9; one type, three instances, each instance's `stages` holds all fourteen). Dispersion family `cloud`, `flicker`, `neighbor`, `attract`: two nonpolar molecules as soft electron clouds with moving violet dots; `flicker`: the dots crowd to one end for an instant, a faint δ− there and δ+ at the other; `neighbor`: the neighbor's cloud shifts the same way; `attract`: a dashed gray line forms, fades, and a new one forms (loop while active; one still frame under reduced motion). Dipole family `F2`, `HCl`, `turn`, `face`, `compare`: `F2` two F₂ molecules with no δ labels and dashed gray only; `HCl` two HCl molecules, δ+ on H (warm) and δ− on Cl (teal); `turn`: they rotate so δ+ faces δ−; `face`: the dashed warm-to-teal line and the label "dipole-dipole forces"; `compare`: the label line "Electrons per molecule" with chips "F₂ 18", "HCl 18" and the label line "Boiling point" with chips "F₂ −188.1 °C", "HCl −85.1 °C". Hydrogen family `water`, `hbond`, `ammonia`, `methane`, `four`: `water` two water molecules (CPK, 104.5°, lone pairs as translucent violet lobes); `hbond`: a dashed teal line from an H of one to a lone pair on the O of the other, drawn longer than the O–H stick, label "hydrogen bond: about 5 to 10% of a covalent bond"; `ammonia`: two NH₃ with the same kind of line; `methane`: two CH₄, a crossed-out dashed line, label "H on C: no hydrogen bond"; `four`: one water molecule with four neighbors and four dashed lines. Units: each molecule pair (or the center molecule and its neighbors in `four`), each label, each chip row. `meta()`: dispersion `{polar:false, deltaLabels:'instant'}`; dipole `{HCl:{dplus:'H', dminus:'Cl'}, electrons:{F2:18, HCl:18}, bp:{F2:-188.1, HCl:-85.1}}`; hydrogen `{water:{donor:'H on O', acceptor:'lone pair on O'}, ammonia:{donor:'H on N', acceptor:'lone pair on N'}, methane:'none', waterMax:4}`.
5. **`bpPlot`** (slides 7 and 9). Stages `axes`, `F2`, `Cl2`, `Br2`, `I2`, `trend`, `trio`, `reveal`, `HCl`. Boiling point (−200 to 200 °C) against electrons per molecule (0 to 110); axis numbers at 36 px or more, ticks every 100 °C and every 20 electrons; points appear one per press with formula and value: F₂ 18, −188.1; Cl₂ 34, −34.0; Br₂ 70, 58.8; I₂ 106, 184.4; `trend`: a dashed line through the halogens and the caption "more electrons, higher boiling point"; `trio`: CH₄, NH₃ and H₂O as three unlabeled points on the 10-electron line with the halogens faint; `reveal`: their labels and values (−161.5, −33.3, 100.0); `HCl`: HCl at 18, −85.1 beside F₂ at 18, −188.1. No "all eight" stage. Units: each point with its label, the axes, the trend caption. `meta()`: `{points:[{f:'F₂', e:18, bp:-188.1, force:'dispersion forces'}, …]}` for the points visible at the current stage, with `force` `dispersion forces`, `dipole-dipole forces` or `hydrogen bonding` (CH₄ dispersion, NH₃ hydrogen bonding, H₂O hydrogen bonding, HCl dipole-dipole, halogens dispersion).
6. **`forceDecider`** (slide 10). Stages `NH3/polar`, `NH3/H`, `NH3/lone`, `NH3/strongest`, `CO2/polar`, `CO2/H`, `CO2/strongest`, `HBr/EN`, `HBr/polar`, `HBr/H`, `HBr/strongest`. Four boxes in the 4.1 `cerBuilder` manner titled "Polar?", "H on N, O or F?", "Lone pair on N, O or F?", "Strongest attraction", filling one per press with the wording of `lessons/4-5.md` (triplet 1): NH₃ yes (trigonal pyramidal, Lesson 4.4) → yes (H on N) → yes → hydrogen bonding; CO₂ no (linear, the pulls cancel) → no (no H) → dispersion forces; HBr `EN` shows the label line "Electronegativity (pull on electrons)" with chips "Br 2.96", "H 2.20", "difference 0.76" → polar yes → H on Br, not N, O or F → dipole-dipole forces. A small molecule drawing beside the boxes (CPK, δ labels where polar). Units: each box, the drawing, the chip row. `meta()`: `{molecule, answers:{polar, H, lone, strongest}}` for the current molecule, with `strongest` exactly `hydrogen bonding`, `dispersion forces`, `dipole-dipole forces`.
