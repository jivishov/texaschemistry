# Brief 02: The 5.1 animations and the 5.1 deck (one lean step)

Step: 02 · Kind: animations and deck · Lesson: 5.1 · Effort: extra-high · Time box: none for the build; the bench comes first and the deck is not started until every stage passes on the bench

## Goal
`unit5/slides-5-1.html` exists, built only by the kit from `parts/lessons/5-1/`, and is the deck of `workshop/plan/5-1.md` section 5: 28 slides, period 1 slides 1 to 14 and period 2 slides 15 to 28, eleven checks on slides 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13, 16, 17, 18, 19, every frame with its cue, label and note, both periods at 47 minutes or less, the words slide with the eight Spanish cognates and the Unit 5 legend, the eleven animations of plan section 4 placed as section 5 says, every rule of `workshop/RULES.md` kept, the overlap check clean. The suite passes in the light theme with the matrix off, except the cross-check lines that read the blueprint's numbers (updated in step 03). The sheets, the run-sheet and the deck go to the teacher.
