3. **`conductTester`** (slides 15, 16, 17). Tester family: `tester`, then `<s>/predict`, `<s>/close`, `<s>/zoom` for `NaCl-s`, `NaCl-l`, `NaCl-aq`, `Cu`, `sugar`, `water`, `KCl-l`, `CaCl2-s`. Units: the circuit, the beaker, the magnified view, the readout line. `meta()`: tester `{sample, state, chargedParticles, freeToMove, lit, carriers}` with the table of `lessons/4-5.md` (solid NaCl: Na⁺ and Cl⁻, no, dark; molten NaCl: yes, lights; salt water: yes, lights; sugar water: none, dark; copper: delocalized electrons, yes, lights; distilled water: very few ions, dark; molten KCl: K⁺ and Cl⁻, yes, lights; solid CaCl₂: Ca²⁺ and Cl⁻, no, dark); dissolve `{sample, type, dissolves}` with NaCl ionic yes, sucrose molecular yes, CaCO₃ ionic barely, I₂ molecular slightly, SiO₂ covalent network no, Cu metallic no.
4. **`hammerBlow`** (slide 18). Stages `before`, `blow`, `shift`, `result`. Two blocks side by side, isometric or `Deck.spheres`: copper (gray "+" cores, 128 pm, violet "−" dots drifting among them) and NaCl (Na⁺ 102 pm red "+", Cl⁻ 181 pm blue "−", 282 pm spacing), every sphere at its nearest depth 60 px or more, the two blocks to one scale. `blow`: a hammer strikes the top layers of both. `shift`: the top layers move one spacing; in copper the electrons flow along; in NaCl every shifted ion now sits beside an ion of the same charge and those pairs flash in `--bad`. `result`: copper bends and stays one piece, label "malleable"; NaCl's layers push apart along a flat face and the block splits with a `--bad` crack, label "brittle". Units: each block, the hammer, each result label. `meta()`: `{stage, copper:{offsetSpacings, onePiece:true}, nacl:{offsetSpacings, likeChargePairs, split}, radii:{Na:102, Cl:181, Cu:128}, spacingPm:282}` with `likeChargePairs>0` from `shift` and `split:true` at `result`.
5. **`networkVsMolecules`** (slide 19). Stages `co2`, `sublime`, `quartz`, `heat`, `diamond`. `co2`: six CO₂ molecules (linear, ball-and-stick, CPK, spheres 60 px or more) with dashed gray dispersion lines between neighbors, a thermometer reading "−78.5 °C". `sublime`: the molecules fly off whole, the dashed lines vanish, no liquid forms; label "sublimes at −78.5 °C: no liquid". `quartz`: a 3D fragment of SiO₂ with `Deck.spheres` (every Si bonded to four O, O bridging; no molecule outline; "Si" and "O" on every sphere); label "quartz, SiO₂: one network, no molecules". `heat`: the thermometer climbs past 1000 °C and the network stays; label "to melt it, covalent bonds must break: near 1700 °C". `diamond`: a carbon network fragment; label "diamond: the hardest natural substance". Units: each solid, the thermometer, each label. `meta()`: `{stage, co2:{molecules:6, intactAfterSublime:true, sublimesAt:-78.5}, sio2:{network:true, molecules:0, meltsNear:1700}, diamond:{element:'C', network:true}}`.
