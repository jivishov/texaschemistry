Texas Chemistry · Grade 10

Investigate matter.
Explain change with evidence.

Chemistry connects the structure of particles and their interactions to the properties and changes we can observe. In each unit, you use models, measurements, calculations and data to build a scientific explanation.

Every lesson has an illustrated reading with simulations and checks, a slide deck for class and a set of missions. Each unit closes with a case file: six evidence files about a real situation that end in a recommendation you write.

How to work through a unit

Follow the evidence path

  1. 01

    Read the chapter. Work through the reading at your own pace, try each simulation and answer the checks.

  2. 02

    Follow the slides in class. Your teacher uses the deck to model the ideas and ask questions.

  3. 03

    Complete the missions. Apply the lesson through observations, calculations and decisions based on evidence.

  4. 04

    Open the case file. At the end of the unit, use the same chemistry on a real situation.

Scientific and engineering practices

Use evidence, models and scientific reasoning

These practices run through the whole course. You ask questions, plan investigations, analyze data, construct explanations and evaluate how chemistry connects to society and STEM work.

Texas Chemistry scientific and engineering practices C.1 to C.4
CodeScientific and engineering practice
C.1Ask questions, plan and safely conduct investigations, and use tools and models.
C.2Analyze and interpret data; identify patterns, error and relationships.
C.3Develop explanations based on evidence; communicate and argue from evidence.
C.4Evaluate scientific work and relate research to society and STEM careers.
